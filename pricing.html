Frequently Asked Questions

Can I really learn Japanese with a game?▼

Absolutely! Research shows gamified learning increases retention by 60% compared to traditional methods. NihonQuest's content is aligned with JLPT standards (N5-N1), so you're learning real, practical Japanese while having fun.

Is it suitable for children?▼

Yes! NihonQuest is designed for ages 6 and up. The Family plan includes parental controls, progress monitoring, and kid-friendly content filters. Many families play together!

What happens after the free trial?▼

After your 7-day trial (Pro) or 14-day trial (Family), you'll be charged the plan price. You can cancel anytime before the trial ends and won't be charged. Your progress is always saved.

Can I switch plans later?▼

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime. If you downgrade to Free, you'll keep your progress but lose access to premium worlds and features until you re-subscribe.

How does the AI conversation partner work?▼

Our AI NPCs adapt to your level and respond naturally in Japanese. Practice ordering food, asking directions, and having everyday conversations. The AI corrects your mistakes and explains grammar in real-time.

Is there a refund policy?▼

Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you're not satisfied, contact us for a full refund, no questions asked.
